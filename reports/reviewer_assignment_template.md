# Bandhoo Reviewer Assignment Template

**Prepared:** 2026-10-21. **Status: ASSIGNMENT PENDING. No reviewer, adjudicator, checker, evaluation lead or source-acquisition owner has been appointed, and the project owner has not approved any assignment.**

Bandhoo remains internal-testing-only. This template records appointments only. It does not record legal decisions. Decisions are recorded in `reports/legal_review_execution_checklist.md` (Stages 2–4) and through `reports/post_review_change_protocol.md`.

Every field below is intentionally blank (`____`). Do not pre-fill names, qualifications, dates or signatures on anyone's behalf. Each appointee fills in or confirms their own entry, and the project owner approves it in Part 3.

## Part 1. Rules for completing this template

1. **Proposed minimum qualifications** come from the Roles table in `reports/legal_review_execution_checklist.md`. They are proposals and must be confirmed or amended by the project owner (PO) in Part 3 before anyone is appointed.
2. **Independence.** QLR 1, QLR 2 and the ADJ must be three different people, and none may have contributed to Bandhoo's code, prompts, corpus or benchmark labels. The SC must not be the author of the change or record being checked. The EL must not be a Bandhoo developer.
3. **Qualification evidence** means a document the PO has actually seen, such as an enrolment certificate or an institutional appointment. Record what was seen and when; do not store copies of personal documents in this repository.
4. **Conflicts** must be declared before any work starts. If a conflict arises later, the person stops work on the affected items and the PO records the replacement.
5. Do not record personal contact details or identity numbers here beyond what the PO decides is necessary.
6. Changing an appointment means adding a new dated entry. Earlier entries are never overwritten.
