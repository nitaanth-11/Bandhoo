# Bandhoo Source Verification Matrix

**Prepared:** 2026-10-20, closing blocker RB-01 (pending maintainer check). **For:** qualified Indian criminal-law reviewers (QLR), the adjudicator (ADJ), the evaluation lead (EL), Bandhoo maintainers (MNT) and the project owner (PO).

**Bandhoo remains internal-testing-only.** This matrix records **source matching only**: whether a claim or passage agrees with a source document actually inspected. A match with one copy is not legal verification, and a mismatch is not by itself proof that Bandhoo is legally wrong. Interpretation is a separate question, marked "interpretation unresolved", and left to the reviewers.

Identifiers are reused from `reports/release_blocker_register.md` (RB-xx) and `reports/legal_review_checklist.md` (LR-xx). Sub-items first identified in this matrix are labelled `<parent>.a`, `<parent>.b` and marked **(new)**.

### 1.3 Status values

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed against available copy** | The claim or passage agrees with the inspected source copy. Not a legal verification. |
| **Discrepancy observed** | The claim or passage differs from the inspected copy on manual reading. Not by itself proof of a legal error. |
| **Suspected difference** | Automated comparison only, or the difference may be an extraction artefact. |
| **Source unavailable** | No authoritative source inspected. |
| **Interpretation unresolved** | The text is available, but applying it requires a legal judgment that no qualified reviewer has made. |
